Real-time Crowd Density Measurement and Risk Detection to Prevent Crowd Crushes

In the Itaewon crowd crush on October 29, 2022, 159 people lost their lives in a narrow alley. Map and navigation apps gave no warning that the area was dangerous, so people kept walking in. This project builds a map that lets pedestrians see danger and avoid it themselves.

How it works

Vision AI pilot (YOLO11)

In a sparse scene YOLO found 4 of 4 people in 0.2 s, but in a packed crowd most people were hidden behind each other. So the design combines YOLO for sparse scenes and a crowd-counting model for dense ones.

Try it

Search for a station near you. Set the scenario to "Halloween weekend" and the time to 21:00 to see danger zones blink, then tap a place in the safety guide to jump to that date and time on the map. Press "Auto demo" at the top to see every feature in order.

※ Densities on this screen are simulated for the demo; the real system uses CCTV video analysis.
